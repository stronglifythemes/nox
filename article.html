How to run an access review that finishes

Access piles up quietly as people change roles. A regular review keeps permissions matched to the work people do today.

Permissions are easy to grant and easy to forget. Over time, accounts collect access to systems their owners no longer use.


This note shows how we help teams review access without stalling their work. To learn more about Nox, visit our about page.



Start with what matters most


Reviewing everything at once rarely finishes. Begin with:


  • Systems that hold sensitive data.
  • Accounts with administrator rights.
  • Access granted to contractors and partners.


Ask the right owners


Security teams can list who has access, but managers know who still needs it. Send each review to the person who can answer.




Every permission should have a reason and an owner.





Laying out roles and permissions side by side makes gaps easier to spot.




Close the loop


When the answers come back:


  • Remove access that nobody can justify.
  • Record why the remaining access is needed.
  • Schedule the next review before closing this one.

Each review gets faster when the last one left a clear record behind.
